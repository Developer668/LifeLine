About the project

LifeLine is a phone-based AI assistant that provides official weather and earthquake information and helps coordinate requests to trusted contacts.

Built for calls and SMS, it requires no app and keeps contact requests subject to participant consent and the caller’s approval.

Alert sources: National Weather Service and USGS.

Unmonitored prototype. LifeLine does not dispatch emergency services, diagnose, confirm safety, or guarantee assistance. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
